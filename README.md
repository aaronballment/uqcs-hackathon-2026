# uqcs-hackathon-2026

This hackathon project brings graphs to life by rendering your equations through your phone using augmented reality.

All you need to do is scan your equation, wait for a few seconds and its graph will be brought to life within your phone.

This Project was built by the Cameron, Aadi, Nirvan and Aaron.

AI was used for assistance in HTML, 3D AR rendering, and JS front end.

Thankyou to UQCS for hosting the event!
